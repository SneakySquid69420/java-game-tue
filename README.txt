To run our game, launch the NEEDSTOBEFILLEDIN file where you can choose whether to start singleplayer and play against a bot
or play multiplayer where you can play against someone else on a different device.

What to test:
In our game you can test various things, let's start with singleplayer. 
- First of all you can test all the buttons (some of them will be grayed out if you can't use them right now)
- Then you can test if the round will end once the game is over and there is a winner or it is a draw, depending
on what cards are on the table and in the hand. 
- The bot will randomly raise if it can and will always call a raise of the player. The amount of the raise will
is determined by the value of the bot's hand. He can raise by €20, €40, €60 and €80.
- Then once the round is over, a Play Again button will appear where raise used to be and the bot's cards will be
displayed so you yourself can also check who won the round. Once you click the Play Again button it will launch a 
new game with new cards but the same amount of money. You can keep playing till either you or the bot is out of 
money, then it will display either you won or you lost everything.

Next let's see the multiplayer.
To set up the multyplayer you click multiplayer on the first screen to start the multiplayer.
- First you can connect using different devices running a host on one of the devices by clicking NEEDSTOBEFILLEDIN and joining
that session on the other device by clicking NEEDSTOBEFILLEDIN.
- Then you can test the same things for the singleplayer, but instead of instantly seeing an updated version (of what
in the singleplayer the bot did automatically), it now waits for the other player to do their action.
- You can now play a full game of poker while on a different device.
